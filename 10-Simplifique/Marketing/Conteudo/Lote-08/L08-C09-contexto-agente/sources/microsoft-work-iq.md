# Work IQ: Business and workplace intelligence in the flow of work 

 By James Oleinik, Partner Director of Product Management, Microsoft Dataverse and Work IQ 

- IT decision maker​

## Products

Work IQ now grounds Microsoft Copilot and agents in your core business data from Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Microsoft Power Platform. Work IQ reasons across the productivity and business data that matters to your business. It’s available in preview starting September 30, 2026, with rollout continuing throughout October 2026.

Enlarge

By connecting and modeling your business once, you create a shared foundation for Copilot and agents: what your data means, how records relate, and which business rules guide the next step. You can then reuse that understanding and governed actions across Copilot and agent experiences without rebuilding context for every agent.

Business applications data and workflows in Work IQ shift the starting point from navigating apps to expressing what you need to accomplish. Your business applications remain the systems of record, but their data and processes are modelled into valuable, reusable business understanding and context made available through Copilot and agents.

Consider a sales lead preparing for a customer renewal. The opportunity looks healthy in the sales system, but Work IQ can bring additional business context to the picture. An unresolved service issue and recent customer email communications reveal that resolving the issue is critical to the renewal. Instead of simply retrieving records, Copilot can use that shared business understanding to help the sales lead determine what needs attention and what to do next.

The same pattern extends far beyond sales. Work IQ can bring business understanding and action to scenarios that span sales, service, finance, operations, and supply chain.

## Users can ask about the business in Copilot and agents

The sales lead starts in Copilot Home, where Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Service, the company’s contract management apps, and Microsoft 365 context are all available. Instead of choosing an app or assembling reports, they begin with a simple business question: “Which upcoming renewals need my attention, and what should I do next?”

Copilot Home connects the opportunity across three separate systems of record and returns a clear, actionable response.

- Copilot identifies a renewal that appears healthy in Dynamics 365 Sales but needs attention when viewed across the business.
- Dynamics 365 Customer Service shows an unresolved equipment issue, and the custom equipment app links that issue to a site covered by the renewing contract.
- Recent customer emails and a Microsoft Teams meeting indicate that resolving the issue is a condition of renewal. The conversation continues but there’s no clear action plan.
